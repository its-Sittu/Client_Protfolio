A luxury, executive corporate portfolio website created for **Anurag Tiwari** (Civil Engineer | M.Tech – Construction Management, KIIT University) & **Managing Director of M/s M.S. Construction** (*Build Plan • Grow.*), Medininagar, Palamu, Jharkhand, India.

## 🏗️ Design & Aesthetic Highlights
- **Theme**: Luxury Corporate Executive + Heavy Infrastructure Engineering
- **Color Palette**: 
  - **Obsidian & Deep Navy**: `#060D17` (Obsidian Base), `#0B192C` (Deep Navy), `#10233D` (Surface Navy)
  - **Imperial Gold & Amber**: `#D4AF37` (Imperial Gold), `#F59E0B` (Amber Glow), `#FFE082` (Light Gold)
  - **White & Slate**: High-contrast, clean typography
- **Typography**: Signature luxury serif **Playfair Display** paired with **Inter** for dense technical data.
- **Hero Photography**: High-resolution industrial construction image featuring yellow tower crane and dense steel rebar framework, with zero text overlap.
- **Interactive Tools**: Live Highway & Infrastructure Feasibility Estimator (IRC:37 & IRC:58 standards) with one-click presets.
- **Components**: Frosted glassmorphism stat cards, top reading progress indicator, blueprint geometric grid, interactive project specifications modal, executive CV viewer with clean `@media print` PDF support, and toast notifications.
